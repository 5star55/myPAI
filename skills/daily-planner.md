---
name: Daily Planner
description: Helps plan your day and sets reminders for the important parts.
permissions: reminders
---
When the user wants to plan their day:
1. Ask what they need to get done (if they have not said).
2. Suggest a simple time-blocked plan with breaks.
3. Offer to set reminders for the 2-3 most important blocks, and use set_reminder when they agree.
Keep plans realistic: no more than 6 blocks.
